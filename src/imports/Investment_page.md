How It Works

## From _Purchase to Deed_

Your share in Hurrem Palace is a permanent, legally registered ownership interest in the property. Here is how your ownership journey unfolds.

01

Choose your package. Pay by instalment or in full. Receive a QR-authenticated payment receipt immediately.

02

### Digital Certificates

Receive your Land Share Certificate and Deed of Agreement — accessible via your secure online profile.

03

### Sub-Kabala Deed

Every three months, sold shares are formally registered through sub-kabala deed — full legal title in your name.

04

### Profit & Perks

Once the hotel opens, receive your annual profit, activate your stay entitlements, and access your Smart Card benefits.

Profit Structure

## How Your _Returns Work_

### 15–20% Estimated Annual Halal Profit

After the hotel commences operations, shareholders earn an estimated annual profit of 15–20% from hotel revenue, distributed proportionally to their shareholding. Profits are halal — generated through legitimate hospitality operations.

Unused annual stay nights are not forfeited — they are calculated and added to your profit for that year, increasing your annual return.

### Investor Dashboard

Log in to the company's secure software platform to view your current share value, download deed documents, track accumulated profit, and watch live project footage.

All transactions — including share purchase, transfer, and profit tracking — can be managed online from anywhere in the world.

* Profit figures are projected estimates based on anticipated hotel operations. Actual revenue may be higher or lower depending on occupancy and market conditions.
