This page is the **emotional and cultural foundation** of Hurrem Palace.

# CHAPTER 02 — THE VISION

Title:

## **A Dream for Bangladesh**

Use the existing company story here, but break it into short visual chapters instead of one large block of text.

### Content structure

**01 — The Need**

Bangladesh has enormous tourism potential, but the project vision argues that the country lacks an iconic destination capable of drawing international visitors simply because of its identity and architecture.

**02 — The Idea**

Create an internationally credible five-star destination.

**03 — The Identity**

Introduce Ottoman architectural heritage and artistry to Bangladesh.

**04 — The Ambition**

Build a destination rather than simply a hotel.

**05 — The Legacy**

Create something future generations can identify with.

The last three concepts are closely aligned with the supplied story content.

### Visual treatment

Each chapter occupies approximately one viewport.

Example:

01                         A DREAM

                           FOR BANGLADESH

  

                  [PALACE DETAIL]

Then the next chapter transforms into:

02                         A PALACE

                           WITH PURPOSE

This is where the paradox effect should begin becoming noticeable.
